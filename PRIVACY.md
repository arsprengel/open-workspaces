# Privacy policy

Open Workspaces does **not** collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- Your workspaces, saved tabs, resources, notes, tasks and settings are stored only in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`).
- The extension makes no network requests of its own. It has no servers, analytics, telemetry, ads or remote code.
- Site icons are read from the browser's local favicon cache (`favicon` permission).
- Exported backups are files you save yourself; they never leave your computer unless you share them.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes its stored data.

The `tabs` and `tabGroups` permissions are used only to save and restore your tabs and tab groups as workspaces.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
